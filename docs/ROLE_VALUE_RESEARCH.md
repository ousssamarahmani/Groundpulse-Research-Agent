# Role-specific value research

## NASA-derived workflow signals

NASA’s small-spacecraft ground-data and mission-operations guidance describes the ground segment as a critical part of end-to-end science data return. It identifies the ground station terminal, mission operations center, science operations center, and ground-data storage/network as distinct elements of a typical small-satellite ground architecture. The same guidance highlights frequency selection, link budgets, licensing, station visibility, telemetry/commanding, and end-to-end compatibility testing as practical engineering concerns.

## Product implications

| User | Immediate question | Useful GroundPulse output |
|---|---|---|
| CubeSat analyst | What does the current public orbit record support for planning? | Freshness, epoch, mean motion, derived period, propagation/contact-planning inputs, and explicit telemetry gaps. |
| Satellite / mission engineer | Which subsystem conclusions are supported and what evidence is missing? | ADCS/GNC, EPS, communications, thermal/structures, and operations evidence matrix with next evidence requests. |
| Space researcher | Which sources can answer the question and how reproducible is the result? | Source ledger, retrieval timestamps, citations, normalized fields, derivations, and a downloadable research package. |
